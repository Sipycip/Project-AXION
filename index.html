Building Intelligence Into the Physical World.

Project AXION is developing intelligent humanoid robotics designed to extend what humans can accomplish in demanding environments.

A long-horizon engineering project for capable machines.

AXION brings humanoid robotics, artificial intelligence, computer vision, autonomous systems, mechanical engineering, automation, and advanced control into one developing physical platform. The focus is real engineering: designing, building, testing, and learning toward robots that can eventually do useful work beyond software alone.


The research horizon includes environments such as space exploration, remote construction, industrial work, and difficult assistance tasks. These are areas of investigation and long-term ambition—not claims of current capability.
